AI Job Seeker by LynkZone ("the Extension") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you use the Extension.

---

### 1. Data Collection and Storage
- **Local-First Architecture:** All your personal profile information (such as your full name, email, phone number, work experience, education, and national identity credentials) is stored **locally on your own device** using Chrome's secure storage API (`chrome.storage.local`).
- **No Data Harvesting or Selling:** LynkZone does not collect, sell, rent, or trade your personal data, job search history, or autofill entries with any third-party advertisers or brokers.

---

### 2. Permissions Justification
- **`activeTab` & `scripting`:** Used strictly to detect form input fields on the active webpage (such as LinkedIn, Indeed, Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, and university admission portals) and fill them only when you explicitly click the "Autofill" button.
- **`storage`:** Used to save your job application profile, custom answers, and local usage counters directly on your device.
- **Host Permissions (`https://*/*`):** Required so the autofill helper script can be executed on any legitimate job application webpage you choose to visit.

---

### 3. AI Processing & Third-Party Services
- When you use the optional **AI Custom Question Copilot** or **ATS Cover Letter Generator**, only the specific question prompt and relevant job context are processed to produce your response.
- Online card payments are securely processed by Stripe or directly coordinated via WhatsApp. Your payment card numbers never pass through or touch LynkZone extension storage.

---

### 4. User Rights and Data Control
- You maintain 100% control over your data.
- You can edit, export (JSON), or delete your profile information and tracked applications at any time from the Extension's **Settings** tab.
- Uninstalling the Extension instantly purges all locally stored data from your device.
